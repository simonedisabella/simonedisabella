**MSc Economics & Finance, University of Milano-Bicocca — Expected March 2027**

I use this GitHub to keep clean, public versions of selected quantitative-finance coursework and research. Most completed work here is in **R** and **MATLAB**; I am adding Python as I work through a dedicated study track.

My main interests are financial time series, portfolio and risk modelling, derivatives, fixed income, and empirical research where the validation design matters as much as the model.

## Selected work

### [Advanced Derivatives Market Analysis](https://github.com/simonedisabella/advanced-derivatives-market-analysis)
Group coursework in **MATLAB** on EURO STOXX 50 options, implied volatility, Monte Carlo pricing, a classroom VSTOXX approximation, EUR OIS bootstrapping and a callable credit-equity-linked bond.

### [Financial Time-Series Econometrics](https://github.com/simonedisabella/financial-time-series-econometrics)
Individual coursework in **R** across 20 exercises: return diagnostics, ARMA/ARIMA, unit-root testing, ARCH/GARCH/EGARCH, volatility forecasting and VaR/Expected Shortfall. The public version documents the corrections made during a later audit rather than hiding them.

### [Financial Market Risk Modelling](https://github.com/simonedisabella/financial-market-risk-modelling)
Group coursework in **R** covering Newey-West inference, AR-GARCH simulation, Variance-Gamma marginals with a Gaussian copula, and a CreditRisk+-style loss model with Student-t dependence.

### [Adaptive Asset Allocation](https://github.com/simonedisabella/adaptive-asset-allocation)
Group coursework in **MATLAB** comparing equal weighting, risk-based allocation, momentum, trend-following and volatility targeting, with a weekly adaptive-allocation extension.

### [Optimal Capital Allocation](https://github.com/simonedisabella/optimal-capital-allocation)
Group coursework in **MATLAB** applying the quadratic capital-allocation framework of Dhaene et al. to empirical VaR/TCE and rolling allocation analysis for a five-equity portfolio.

## Python — research in development

### Master's thesis — research design
Planned thesis on **21-trading-day tail-risk forecasting for SPY**. The design compares historical and filtered-historical approaches with a GARCH/EVT specification, then tests whether VIX-based information improves physical VaR/Expected Shortfall forecasts. The risk forecasts are intended to feed a no-leverage SPY/cash risk-budgeting rule. **The research design is in progress; no empirical results are claimed yet.**

### BTP Macro-ML Risk Lab — planned
Planned Python project on **one-month-ahead changes in the Italy-Germany 10-year sovereign spread**. The design uses expanding-window evaluation, zero-change and AR(1) benchmarks, Elastic Net and HistGradientBoosting, a market-only versus market-plus-macro comparison, a CISS-based stress slice, and a small DV01 module to translate forecast errors into bond-risk terms. **The design is complete; implementation has not started.**
